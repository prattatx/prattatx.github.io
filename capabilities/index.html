Capabilities / The ground I can cover

The audit is the offer. This is the range behind it.

Research

Generative and evaluative. Field studies, contextual inquiry, usability testing, benchmark programs at scale.
Knowledge elicitation
Cognitive task analysis and stimulated recall, for work experts cannot fully describe on demand.
Job analysis
Identifying what performance is in a role, then finding the indicators that track it.
Measurement and validation
Instrument design, criterion-related validity studies, survey programs.

Design and systems

Deciding which tasks belong to people and which to machines, and at what level of autonomy.
User experience design
Interface and interaction design for complex decision systems and consumer products.
AI product strategy
What to build, what to validate first, and what the machine should decide.

Intellectual property

Invention triage
Surfacing what is novel in a body of work and cutting it to a shortlist counsel can act on.
Patent development workshops
Working sessions that end in disclosures rather than a deck.
