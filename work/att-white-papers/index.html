Work / Case study / Thought leadership / 2017

Giving a 200,000-person company shared language for AI

I was lead author of an enterprise automation white paper and the Data Powered series. I convened engineers and senior leaders, brought their views into one account, and wrote it.

Role
Lead author. I convened contributors from engineering and senior leadership, ran the multi-author review, and wrote the synthesis.
Context
Chief Data Office of a Fortune 10 telecom.
Kind of work
Writing, Strategy
Date
2017 and following

The problem

A large enterprise pushing into AI, automation, and data-driven decision-making lacked shared language and frameworks for it. Many smart people held pieces of the picture across different teams, but there was no authoritative, common reference to align strategy and investment.

The approach

My method was the same across the papers, including an enterprise automation white paper and the Data Powered series: convene the right experts, align them, and write the synthesis.

  • Bring engineers and leaders together and surface what each actually knew.
  • Use human-factors and facilitation techniques to converge many viewpoints into one coherent narrative.
  • Author and structure the synthesis, including durable frameworks such as a five-milestone automation maturity model.

The papers carried many authors, and the company ordered bylines by seniority. I wrote the papers and ran the review across the authors.

The outcome

Published internal thought leadership that gave the organization shared language and frameworks for AI and automation, used to align strategy and orient teams. The papers became a common reference point across teams.

How I led it

  • I convened engineers and senior leaders and drew out what each of them knew.
  • I ran the review across the authors and resolved their differences into one narrative.
  • I wrote durable frameworks into the papers, including a five-milestone automation maturity model.
