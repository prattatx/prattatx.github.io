Keeping a competitive picture current

A competitive-intelligence program I directed as CEO for an emerging consumer-AI category: comparable deep dives, a positioning map, a dashboard the team used, and a weekly scan to keep it current.

Role
Founder and CEO. I directed the program, chose the competitors and the frame, and used the result to set where the company would compete.
Context
Emerging consumer-AI category. My own company.
Kind of work
Research, Strategy
Date
2026

The problem

A fast-moving category with well-funded entrants and no settled positioning. The company needed a rigorous, independently sourced read of the landscape, and a way to keep it current, to decide where to differentiate.

The approach

I set this up as a standing program with a weekly cadence.

  • Shortlist discipline. A defined set of competitors, each chosen to stress-test the positioning thesis from a different angle: direct analog, best-funded peer, adjacent category, wildcard.
  • Structured deep dives. Each competitor analyzed on a consistent frame (buyer, wedge, funding, technology approach, defensibility) so they could be compared.
  • Positioning map. Defined the axes that actually separated the field and placed every player on them, exposing the unoccupied position.
  • Interactive dashboard so the team could explore the analysis and reuse it.
  • Ongoing monitoring. A weekly news-scan cadence so the picture stayed live as competitors raised, launched, and pivoted.

The outcome

A living competitive picture that turned a noisy field into a clear, defensible wedge and an ongoing intelligence cadence, used to align the team and prioritize where to compete.

How I led it

  • I chose the shortlist so that each competitor tested the positioning thesis from a different angle.
  • I set one frame for every deep dive, so the team compared like with like.
  • I used the map to align the team on where to compete.

Method-only. Competitor names, strategic conclusions, threat assessments, and partner targets removed. Focus is the analysis method.
