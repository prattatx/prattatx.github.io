The audit

One workflow, watched and allocated

Your team has AI. What nobody has answered is which parts of the work it should be doing, and who on your team is actually getting anything out of it. I answer both, by watching the work.

Not a survey and not a workshop. I watch people do their real jobs with the tools they really have, and ask the follow-up questions that surface what they could not have told you in a meeting. The tacit part never reaches a requirements doc, because nobody knows to say it out loud. That is the part your model is missing.

Passion
The drive to reach for new capability.
Agency
What they hand to the machine, and whether they drive the interaction.
Rigor
Whether they verify, and whether they catch confident-sounding errors.
Taste
The quality of their judgment about what comes back.

This is an audit, not a hiring test. PART measures dispositions toward AI-assisted work and is built to inform how you deploy AI across a team you already have. It is not validated as a pre-employment selection instrument and must not be used to make hiring decisions.

Rigor is scored in two parts on purpose. The risky profile is not the person who distrusts the tool. It is the person who acts confidently and verifies poorly, and a single score hides them.

What you get: an account of how the work actually happens, including where your current spec is wrong about it. The allocation, every task, human or machine, at what level, with the reasoning. The prompts and specs that encode it, usable this week. And a read on where your team is strong and where it is exposed.

$5,000 for one workflow. Larger engagements are scoped.
