Founder

Fyve Health, 2025 to 2026

I co-founded Fyve Health in 2025 and ran it as CEO for a year. We built Tessa, a memory and caregiving companion for families with aging parents and for care communities.

Tessa worked by sound. It had no camera, and it processed audio on the device in the home. The idea came from years as a long-distance caregiver for my own parents.
