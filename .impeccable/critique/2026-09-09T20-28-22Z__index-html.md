## Overall Impression

A well-built document wearing a landing page's clothes, and the clothes are the wrong size for the only
visitor who shows up.

The craft is real and measurable: 26 desktop and 22 mobile tab stops each with a visible focus ring, zero
contrast pairs below threshold, reduced-motion honored, nothing below the 11px floor, no horizontal overflow,
one h1, no skipped heading levels, clean content rules.

The biggest opportunity is a deletion followed by a promotion. The page's job is to confirm fast, then ask.
It currently introduces at length, confirms 2400px in, and asks at 3967px. Invert that and most of the
priority list evaporates.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The conversion is invisible for the first 91% of the page

What: cal.com appears exactly once in index.html, line 231, in the contact block. Measured, it first paints at
y=3967 of 4325px desktop (91.8% down, 4.4 viewport-heights) and y=5106 of 5950px mobile (85.8% down, 6.0
viewport-heights). The only other instance is an 11px footer link measuring 60x18px. Meanwhile .masthead is
position: sticky; top: 0, present at every scroll position, spending all six slots on the wordmark and five
browse links.

Why it matters: PRODUCT.md states the visitor arrives already warm, mid-diligence, and that a booked call is the
only success condition. A warm visitor's confidence peaks early and decays with scroll. This page makes them
travel past five engagement types, three case studies, the full record, and a pitch for a different company
before it offers the one action it wants. Every visitor who confirms what they came to confirm and then leaves
is a conversion the page architecturally refused.

Fix: Put Book time in the sticky masthead as the only filled control in the site chrome. Point the hero's
.btn-primary (index.html:34) at site.calendar_url and demote "How I work" to .btn-ghost. Add one inline
.link-out to cal.com at the foot of the record section, where confidence peaks.

Suggested command: $impeccable layout

### [P1] `2 / Acquired by other companies` reads as two company exits

What: index.html:49-50 renders 2 at 44px desktop / 28px mobile, labeled "Acquired by other companies", immediately
right of "358 / Granted U.S. patents" and about 250px below the hero line "Founder twice over." The record section
resolves it correctly as patents ("Now owned by other companies: 2", index.html:165) 3100px later.

Why it matters: CLAUDE.md rule 5 and PRODUCT.md both carry a standing constraint: wisely.io is not an exit, it

Fix: Relabel to "Patents now owned by other companies", or replace outright. "226 / Still in force" is unambiguous,
stronger, and says something the raw grant count does not.

Suggested command: $impeccable clarify

### [P1] A publication count PRODUCT.md says not to cite ships twice on the highest-traffic page

What: index.html:58 shows "9 / Peer-reviewed papers" and index.html:179 shows "Peer-reviewed publications: 9".
CLAUDE.md's clearance section and README both say six. PRODUCT.md flags the conflict as unresolved and says
explicitly not to cite a count until settled.

Why it matters: cited twice, and the second instance sits in the same block as "Verified 2026-08-07", which implies
the record was checked against a source. A stress-tester who finds the six will discount the 358 too, because the
verification stamp now covers a number that disagrees with the project's own clearance note. This is the failure
mode CLAUDE.md rule 1 exists to prevent, and it is currently shipping.

Fix: Settle the count against the clearance record, then make all four documents agree. Until then the stat rail
slot is better spent on "226 / Still in force".

Suggested command: $impeccable clarify

### [P2] The stat rail can render as blank paper

What: .stats.reveal sits at top 835-849px in a 900px desktop viewport. site.js:135 observes it with
rootMargin '0px 0px -8% 0px' and threshold 0.08, putting the effective trigger boundary at 828px, essentially at
the element. Across six identical loads it stayed at opacity 0 once and reached 1 five times. With an
IntersectionObserver present that never delivers, it stays at 0 permanently. The full-page desktop screenshot
captured it invisible.

Why it matters: design.md:237 calls this "the one orchestrated entrance per page," attached to the single strongest
asset on the site. When it does not fire, the referred founder sees a blank band where 358 should be and scrolls on.
The failure is silent and leaves no trace.

Fix: Exclude the first .reveal from the observer and let it paint visible. An entrance that only fires once you have
already seen the element is not doing work. Or add a window.load fallback that force-adds .in to anything still
unobserved.

Suggested command: $impeccable animate

### [P2] Six exits at the single decision point, one of them a personal Gmail

What: index.html:230-240 offers Book time, Email me, james@fyve.dev, jhp96001@gmail.com, LinkedIn, ORCID. The
advisory address appears twice. PRODUCT.md Principle 3: every page should leave a verifying founder one obvious step

Why it matters: two identically-shaped 49px buttons split the conversion and four text links dilute it further.

Suggested command: $impeccable distill

Ordered by how close they came to the priority list.

- A-E engagement codes ship with no key. index.html:75-103 render A-E in accent; the proof cards reuse the same
  letters as tags at :125-137, ~900px later. Nothing defines them. They are the Career Management workstation's
  internal Portfolio Index codes leaked onto a public surface, and accent color is spent on the least informative
  characters on the page. Keep them in data- attributes for tooling, give the tags a word.
- Hover and focus are not paired on .btn, .link-out, or .nav a. design.md:256 names this as drift explicitly. Both
  assessments found it independently, on different elements.
- No skip link in the layout. One line, affects every page.
- index.html:22 carries the page's only inline style, style="color:var(--text)", putting hero body copy at --ink
  (14.75:1) where the system reserves --ink-2 (9.53:1) for reading. The two hero paragraphs measure visibly different
  colors with no apparent reason, so it reads as a mistake rather than emphasis.
- --rail: 88px reserves permanent body padding at >=1000px for a decorative waveform, while the conversion has no
  room in the masthead.
- 620px of empty paper sits right of the hero ledes at 1440px, the cheapest available place to put the ask above the
  fold at zero structural cost.
- Both button pairs share .hero-actions, so the conversion button is geometrically identical to an in-page anchor
  jump. Neither Book time nor Email me signals that it leaves the site.
- The mobile stat rail shows 2 of 4 stats above the fold, and they are 358 and the ambiguous 2.
- Chunking runs long in three places: Practice ships 5 rows, the patents dl ships 6, the footer ships 7 links.
- design.md contradicts itself on section heads, separately from the two already-tracked stale items: line 313 says
  "mono-caps label on a hairline," line 197 says labels become real Inter headings "not 12px mono caps." Shipped is
  Inter 20px/600, matching :197. Line 332's "Cal Sans and Inter" is stale v1 text inside the live "What pages MUST
  share" section.
- Content rules pass clean: zero em dashes, zero en dashes, zero banned words in index.html.
- The portrait rendering as an empty mount in the offline preview is an absolute-path artifact, not a finding.

## Questions to Consider

1. If nobody arrives cold, why is the largest object on the page the one piece of information every visitor already has?
2. design.md:303 says the /patents/ field "is where the site's colour and visual interest come from. It is why the
   chrome is allowed to be quiet." The landing page never shows it. What if the hero IS the 358-mark field, with the
   name at 24px beside it? The referred founder gets her verification in one glance instead of 2400px.
3. Five engagement types, two of which serve the primary user. Would a founder convert faster if B, C and E lived on
   /about/ and the landing page offered exactly A and D?
4. PRODUCT.md says nothing measures whether a visit produced a booking, so every judgment here is an argument rather
   than evidence. The cheapest instrument that settles it is a UTM on the cal.com href plus a GA4 outbound-click
   event. Roughly two lines. Why not add it before changing anything else?
5. "The fit conversation is short and honest." Short is how long? The founder is answering a cost question in order
   to decide, and the page declines to state the cost.
6. The record section is the emotional peak and both its links send the visitor further from the booking. What is the
   argument for that, other than that nobody has looked at where the peak sits?
