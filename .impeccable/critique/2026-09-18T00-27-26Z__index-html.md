## Design Specificity Verdict

**The site is strongly authored. The sale is not, and the seam is visible.**

Everything predating this branch could not be lifted by another consultancy without gutting it:
the fixed-seed signal rail grounded in real sleep-EEG and edge-audio work, the warm hue-75-to-85
paper that refuses `#ffffff`, the inverted pairing where Inter carries structure and Source Serif
4 carries reading, and the record-as-artifact strategy that earns the chrome its quiet. That is a
system with a thesis.

The new material is the opposite. The audit block is five unstyled `<p>` elements at 122ch with
zero margin between them, and a three-column ledger whose third column says "Disposition" four
times. The Engagements ladder below it is the identical component resolving to the identical
grid, `355px 497px 132px`, measured on both. The most commercially important 1,200px of the page
is the least designed real estate on the property.

**Deterministic scan.** 69 findings across the six inlined pages, exit 2. Breakdown:
`cramped-padding` 25, `tight-leading` 21, `all-caps-body` 10, `overused-font` 6, `oversized-h1` 3,
`marketing-buzzword` 2, `tiny-text` 1, `skipped-heading` 1. Every finding emits `line: 0`, so none
name a selector.

Against measurement, most of that is noise. `cramped-padding` claims children sit flush against a
`border-top`; measured section padding is 64.8px on all four pages. `tight-leading` reports ratios
of 0.13x to 0.18x; real body copy measures 1.60 to 1.65 everywhere, and the only sub-1.3 elements
are authored display declarations. `oversized-h1` reports "3168px h1" against a measured 64px, a
parse artifact. `marketing-buzzword` fires twice on "next-generation destroyer program," a literal
Navy program name. `all-caps-body` is the label voice the system sanctions.

Two detector findings survive, and one open item finally closes:

- **`skipped-heading` on `/work/` is real.** `<h1>` at `work/index.html:12` jumps to `<h3>` at
  `work/index.html:35` with no `<h2>`. Identical on `master`, so it predates this branch, and it
  is generator-owned.
- **`wide-tracking` is now closed.** Two rounds open, resolved by enumerating all 21 declarations
  at or above 0.08em in `assets/css/main.css`. **Zero land on body copy.** Thirteen are Inter caps
  label voice, two are mono data, six are mixed-case micro-labels and the wordmark. Move it to the
  accepted list.
- One correction to `ignore.md`: it describes the sanction as "mono-caps label voice," but
  `design.md` v2 says mono is data only and the label voice is Inter caps. The exception text
  describes the v1 system.

**Visual overlays.** None. No browser-automation MCP is exposed in this session, so the
live-server and `[Human]` tab injection path was unavailable. Substituted headless Chrome via
puppeteer-core with direct computed-style measurement and screenshots read back visually. No
user-visible overlay exists in the browser.

## Overall Impression

Two rounds of critique hardened the chrome to a genuinely high standard, and the measurements
prove it: the palette resolves exactly to the documented values, 11px holds as a floor with zero
violations, ten control types return one identical focus ring, one reveal fires per page, reduced
motion collapses spatial motion, both fonts resolve with no silent fallback. **Nothing that rounds
1 and 2 fixed has regressed.**

All three regressions are new material, and all three trace to the same cause: content grew and
the layout was not asked whether it could hold it. Seven nav items in a shell budgeted for five.
Full engagement names in pills sized for single letters. Nine content units in a section that was
a five-row menu. The single biggest opportunity is that the fixes are small: the P0 is one CSS
value, and the worst layout problem is a missing wrapper class.

## What's Working

1. **The mobile nav state machine is correct, which is rare.** Escape closes the panel, resets
   `aria-expanded` to `false`, restores the label to "Menu," and returns focus to the toggle.
   Verified through a real key event at 390px, not a DOM poke. Most sites ship one of those four
   behaviors. This ships all four.
2. **Measurement discipline holds under adversarial checking.** Six control types, one
   `2px solid rgb(79,82,212)` ring at 3px offset, no exceptions. Smallest rendered text exactly
   11px, nothing below. Body copy at 9.53:1 against an 8:1 floor.
3. **`/method/` and `/capabilities/` belong.** Both use the shared `h2.label` head at identical
   20px Inter 600 with identical `-0.2px` tracking, the same hairline, the same right-aligned
   count. `/capabilities/` correctly holds the Index family's even rhythm. They are the
   best-judged new work on the branch.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The masthead CTA is clipped or gone from 681px to 905px

`_layouts/default.html:50` against `assets/css/main.css:869`

**What.** Seven nav links need 584px. Add a 134px wordmark, a 116px CTA and the gaps, and the
masthead has a hard minimum of **906px**. The `.nav-toggle` does not appear until
`max-width: 680px`. That leaves a **225px dead band** where the desktop nav is still on and the
mobile toggle is still off.

Measured, homepage, `.masthead-cta` bounding box against viewport:

| Width | CTA box | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1024 | 884 to 1000 | fine |
| 905 | 790 to 906 | 1px over |
| 834 | 790 to 906 | **72px cut off, reads "Book"** |
| 768 | 790 to 906 | **entirely off-screen**, `elementFromPoint` returns `.shell` |
| 700 | 790 to 906 | CTA gone, and the nav itself now extends to 766 |
| 680 | 564 to 660 | toggle takes over, fine |

`body` computes `overflow-x: clip`, so there is no scrollbar and `scrollWidth` never exceeds
`clientWidth`. **96 of 96 overflow checks report clean.** A static pass and a `scrollWidth` check
both call this page healthy.

**Why it matters.** 768 and 834 are iPad portrait, the most likely device for a referred operator
checking someone between meetings. The masthead is on every page. On `/capabilities/` and
`/work/`, which carry no in-body booking link, it removes the only above-the-fold path to the one
conversion this site has. The GA4 `book_click` event for `utm_content=masthead` cannot fire from
tablet. Booking is still possible via the hero and footer positions on the homepage, so this is
not a total block, but it is a visibly broken masthead sitewide at common widths.

**Fix.** Raise the mobile-nav breakpoint from 680px to 940px at `assets/css/main.css:869` and its
companion at `:884`. One value. Keeps all seven links, keeps all seven booking positions, costs
nothing at desktop. Do not tighten the nav gap instead: the budget is short by 138px at 768, so
tightening only relocates the failure.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

### [P1] The /work/ tag pills are drawn as broken capsules

`assets/css/main.css:664`, fed by `_gen/cases.py:14`

**What.** `.card .tags` is flex, which covers the homepage. `.work-list .item .tags` at
`assets/css/main.css:1150` sets only `margin-bottom`, so it computes `display: block`, and `.tag`
declares no `display` at all. The pills are therefore **inline boxes**. An inline box that wraps
produces two fragments, and the 9999px radius is sliced open at the break.

Measured by `getClientRects().length > 1`: **6 broken pills at 1440px, 9 at 1024, 0 at 768, 10 at
390.** "Strategy sprint," "Expert consult," "Workshops and speaking" and "Fractional / advisory AI
product lead" all split. 768 is clean by coincidence of column width, not by design.

The filter buttons are fine, the card tags are broken. The filter group never wraps inside a
button and never overflows at any width. It takes 1 row at 1440, 2 at 1024 and 768, 4 at 390.

**Fix.** Add `display: inline-block` to `.tag` at `assets/css/main.css:664`, which makes each pill
atomic so it wraps as a unit. Then shorten the labels in `ENGAGEMENTS` at `_gen/cases.py:14`:
"Fractional / advisory AI product lead" is 255px of an 11px pill and is a job title, not a tag.
The full name already lives in the case-study meta grid. Regenerate through the generator; never
touch `work/*/index.html`.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

### [P1] The offer block's paragraphs have no measure cap and no margin

`index.html:80, :120, :126, :135`

**What.** The 68ch caps in the stylesheet are all scoped to `.prose`, `.case-body` and
`.post-body`. The audit block's body copy is bare `<p>` directly inside `.shell`, so it matches
none of them and renders at **1032px, 121.9ch, 79% over the documented cap, with
`margin-bottom: 0px`**. Six adjacent paragraph pairs measure a literal **0px gap**. The only
vertical separation in the entire block comes from `.sec-head`'s 32px and `p.note`'s 32px.

This is the mechanical cause of "it reads as a wall." The block is long, but the structure is the
half that is actually broken: a 55ch lede sits directly on top of a 122ch paragraph with nothing
between them.

**Fix.** Wrap the body copy in `<div class="prose">`, which already carries both the 68ch cap and
the `--s5` rhythm. No new CSS. `/method/` has the same defect on one pair at
`method/index.html:39-47`. Then reassess length: with real separation and a 68ch measure, the
block may not need cutting at all.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] The factor table and the Engagements ladder are the same component, and one has nothing to put in its third column

`index.html:87-113` against `index.html:194-214`

**What.** Both are `.rows.rows--plain` and both resolve to the identical grid,
`355px 497px 132px`. The ladder's third column carries `$5,000`, `Five figures, scoped`,
`Retainer`. The factor table's third column carries **"Disposition" four times**
(`index.html:92, :98, :104, :110`). The audit section head string "One workflow, watched and
allocated" is then repeated verbatim as the opening of the ladder's first row.

They read as the same thing twice, and the ladder is the one that works. A right-aligned meta
column in a ledger promises a comparable quantity per row. The factor table has none, so it ships
a dead column whose only function is to make a glossary look like a price list, 400px above an
actual price list in the same shape.

**Fix.** Give the four PART factors their own treatment: drop the meta column and set them as a
term/definition block. `design.md` explicitly permits row versus card versus table as a per-page
difference, so this stays inside the system. "Disposition" then either goes away or is promoted
once as a lead-in above the four. Leave the ladder exactly as it is.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] The compliance disclaimer is the faintest element in the section it governs

`index.html:115-118` against `assets/css/main.css:1036`

**What.** `.note` renders at 15px in `--text-3` (5.34:1, the muted-label tier) behind a 2px rule.
Everything around it, including the 122ch paragraphs, sits at `--ink`, 14.75:1. The sentence that
keeps PART on the unregulated side of EEOC Uniform Guidelines, NYC LL144 and the EU AI Act is
styled as a footnote the eye is trained to skip. It is correctly placed and incorrectly weighted.
It is also the sentence a nervous buyer most wants to find.

**Fix.** Scope this instance to `--ink-2` (9.53:1) at `--text-base`, keeping the accent rule for
the aside shape. Do not change `.note` globally: its case-study uses are correctly quiet.

**Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`
