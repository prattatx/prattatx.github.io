## Priority Issues

### [P1] The stat rail spends a peak slot on a non-credential and omits the strongest number
index.html:49-52 renders "2 / Patents now owned by other companies" at 44px, while "226 in force",
a fixed fact in PRODUCT.md and CLAUDE.md, is absent from the rail and buried at 2530px. The relabel
in the prior session fixed the ambiguity but left the slot doing no work.
Fix: swap slot 2 to "226 / Active and in force". The 2 already has a home at index.html:161.
Suggested command: $impeccable clarify

### [P1] The rail's labels are cut off by the fold
Measured live at 1440x900: numbers y=856, labels y=908-941, fold y=900. Labels not visible. A
visitor told "hundreds of patents" sees 358 2 17 9 with every label clipped. The fold spends 649px
on the name, two ledes and three buttons.
Fix: reclaim ~60px from the hero (h1 down one step, or tighten .hero bottom padding).
Suggested command: $impeccable layout

### [P2] The mid-page booking added last session is the weakest CTA on the page
index.html:183-185 renders the post-record conversion as .link-out: 15px indigo in a 96x25px hit
area. Every other booking is .btn-primary at 129x49px. Placement is right, treatment is wrong. It
sits at the highest-intent point and is quieter than "All nine case studies" 700px above it.
Fix: make it .btn-primary.
Suggested command: $impeccable layout

### [P2] Two keyboard defects, both one-liners
Verified live: (a) the skip link scrolls but does NOT move focus (activeElement is BODY after
Enter, #main has no tabindex), so the next Tab restarts at the top and it skips nothing.
(b) Escape does not close the mobile nav (.nav retains is-open); site.js binds click only.
The skip link was added last session and reported working on the basis of the hash changing, not
where focus went.
Fix: <main id="main" tabindex="-1">, plus a keydown Escape handler returning focus to .nav-toggle.
Suggested command: $impeccable harden

### [P2] Label tracking applied to sentence-case copy in four places
.stat .k (.11em), .row .meta (.1em), .sec-head .count (.1em), .site-foot (.08em) carry the
uppercase-label tracking with no text-transform. .contact-links .k does it correctly. Wide tracking
on sentence case reduces word-shape legibility at exactly the 11-12px sizes where it matters.
Fix: add the uppercase treatment where the label voice is intended, or drop tracking toward 0 where
the text is a sentence. The footer colophon is the clearest case for dropping it.
Suggested command: $impeccable typeset
