Summary

Cognitive psychologist, founder, and AI product leader with seventeen years building and shipping AI at consumer and enterprise scale. Currently CEO and co-founder of Fyve Health, building privacy-first ambient AI for elder caregiving. Previously Director of AI User Experience in AT&T's Chief Data Office, leading generative AI product design and architecting the enterprise AI transformation: governance, self-service platforms, and workforce strategy adopted across 20+ business units. Named inventor on 358 granted U.S. patents and a top-10 inventor company-wide.
